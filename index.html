About Me

Nicoline Roux

Master's-qualified Architectural Designer

I am a Master's-qualified Architectural Designer with over six years of experience designing and delivering commercial interiors, retail environments, bespoke furniture, and customer-focused spatial experiences. As a founder of The Line Studios and Custom Line Manufacturing, I have led projects from concept development through manufacturing and installation, combining creative design with technical execution.

My expertise lies in creating showroom environments that enhance customer engagement, showcase products effectively, and translate brand identity into memorable retail experiences. With a strong understanding of merchandising, modular display systems, material specification, and construction detailing, I deliver practical, buildable solutions that balance aesthetics, functionality, and commercial objectives.

Our Approach

At The Line, we believe great spaces begin with listening. We work closely with our clients to understand their vision, needs, and budget, ensuring every piece is both functional and thoughtfully designed.

Guided by architectural insight and engineering precision, we focus on quality workmanship, careful material selection, and attention to detail from start to finish.

Above all, our approach is personal. We value long-term relationships, honest collaboration, and going the extra mile to create furniture and spaces our clients truly feel connected to.

Our Philosophy

At The Line, we believe that a space is more than a finished product — it is part of the life lived within a space. Our philosophy is rooted in craftsmanship, integrity, and human connection.

We believe in doing things properly: thoughtful design, honest materials, and precise workmanship. We create spaces that are functional, beautiful, and built to last.

Our philosophy is simple: create meaningful spaces, build lasting relationships, and deliver quality that speaks for itself.

How We Work

The Line Studios Process

Six phases. Zero surprises.

01

Discovery & Briefing

An in-depth consultation to understand your brand identity, operational needs, timeline and budget before a single line is drawn.

02

Concept Development

Mood boards, spatial narratives and material palettes prepared for your approval — translating the brief into a cohesive design vision.

03

Technical Design

Detailed drawings, specifications, BOQs and 3D renders prepared to construction-ready standard and coordinated with consultants.

04

Procurement & Supply

Furniture, fixtures and finishes sourced directly through our store and wholesale network at meaningful savings over retail.

05

Site Execution

Our project managers oversee contractors, run regular site inspections and maintain weekly reporting so you stay informed throughout.

06

Handover & Aftercare

Professional photography, as-built documentation and a 12-month aftercare warranty. We build partnerships, not transactions.
